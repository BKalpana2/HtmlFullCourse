Popular Indoor Plants

1. Money Plant

Money plants are easy to grow and maintain.

Water: When the soil begins to dry.

2. Aloe Vera

Aloe vera is a succulent plant that stores water in its leaves.

Water: When the soil is completely dry.

3. Snake Plant

Snake plants can tolerate low-light conditions.

Water: When the soil has dried out.

Plant Care Tips

  1. Choose a suitable location for your plant.
  2. Provide the right amount of sunlight.
  3. Avoid overwatering.
  4. Use suitable soil and pots with drainage holes.
  5. Remove dead or damaged leaves.

Plant Care Schedule

Plant Sunlight Watering
Money Plant Indirect Light When soil begins to dry
Aloe Vera Bright Light When soil is dry
Snake Plant Low to Bright Indirect Light When soil is dry
