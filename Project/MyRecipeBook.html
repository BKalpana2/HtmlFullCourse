Chocolate Milkshake

Ingredients

  • 1 glass of milk
  • 2 spoons of chocolate powder
  • 2 spoons of sugar
  • Ice cubes

Preparation Steps

  1. Take a glass of milk.
  2. Add chocolate powder and sugar.
  3. Mix everything well.
  4. Add ice cubes.
  5. Pour into a glass and serve.

Fruit Salad

Ingredients

  • Apple
  • Banana
  • Mango
  • Grapes

Preparation Steps

  1. Wash all the fruits.
  2. Cut the fruits into small pieces.
  3. Mix all the fruits in a bowl.
  4. Serve fresh.
