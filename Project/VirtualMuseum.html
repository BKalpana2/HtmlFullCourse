Hall 1: Ancient Civilizations

1. Indus Valley Civilization

The Indus Valley Civilization flourished in parts of present-day Pakistan and northwestern India. Harappa and Mohenjo-daro are among its best-known cities.

Known for: Urban planning, drainage systems, and standardized weights.

2. Ancient Egypt

Ancient Egypt developed along the Nile River. Its civilization is famous for monumental pyramids, hieroglyphic writing, and royal tombs.

Known for: Pyramids, papyrus, and hieroglyphics.

3. Ancient Rome

Ancient Rome influenced architecture, engineering, law, and political institutions across Europe and the Mediterranean.

Known for: Roads, aqueducts, and monumental buildings.

Did You Know?

Discover the story of the printing press

Movable-type printing in Europe made it possible to produce many copies of texts more efficiently, helping knowledge spread to wider audiences.


Discover the story of the World Wide Web

Tim Berners-Lee proposed the World Wide Web in 1989 while working at CERN. It made it easier to access and navigate linked information over the internet.
