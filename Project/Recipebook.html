Recipe 1: Masala Chai

Preparation time: 10 minutes

Serves: 2 people

Ingredients

  • 2 cups water
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 teaspoons tea leaves
  • 2 teaspoons sugar
  • 1 small piece ginger
  • 2 cardamom pods

Steps

  1. Boil water with ginger and cardamom.
  2. Add tea leaves and let it boil for two minutes.
  3. Add milk and sugar.
  4. Boil again, then strain into cups.

Recipe 2: Vegetable Sandwich

Preparation time: 15 minutes

Serves: 1 person

Ingredients

  • 2 bread slices
  • Butter
  • Cucumber slices
  • Tomato slices
  • Onion slices
  • Cheese slice
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Apply butter on both bread slices.
  2. Place cucumber, tomato, and onion slices.
  3. Add cheese, salt, and pepper.
  4. Cover with the second bread slice and serve.
