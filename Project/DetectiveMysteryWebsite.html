Case #007: The Missing Diamond

The Incident

On October 15, at exactly 11:30 PM, a priceless diamond disappeared from Blackwood Mansion.

The mansion was locked from the inside. Four people were present that night, and each person has a different story. Detective Alex Morgan has been assigned to solve the case.

"The truth is hidden in the smallest details."
— Detective Alex Morgan

Meet the Suspects

Suspect 1: Mr. Richard Blackwood

Occupation: Mansion Owner

Statement: "I was reading in my library."

Motive: Recently faced financial problems.

Suspect 2: Emily Carter

Occupation: Personal Assistant

Statement: "I was preparing tea in the kitchen."

Motive: Was recently denied a promotion.

Suspect 3: Daniel Wilson

Occupation: Security Guard

Statement: "I was checking the main entrance."

Motive: Has access to every room in the mansion.

Suspect 4: Sophia Green

Occupation: Family Friend

Statement: "I was sitting in the garden."

Motive: Wanted to buy the diamond.

Evidence Collected

  1. A muddy footprint was found near the display cabinet.
  2. The library clock stopped working at 11:15 PM.
  3. A broken piece of glass was discovered near the garden door.
  4. The security camera stopped recording at 11:20 PM.
  5. A wet umbrella was found inside the library.

Investigation Timeline

Time Event Location
10:30 PM Guests arrived Main Hall
11:15 PM Library clock stopped Library
11:20 PM Security camera stopped Security Room
11:30 PM Diamond discovered missing Display Room

Detective's Investigation Notes

Clue 1: The Muddy Footprint

The footprint suggests that someone may have entered from outside. However, the footprint alone does not identify the suspect.

Clue 2: The Broken Glass

The broken glass was found near the garden door. Investigators must determine whether it was broken before or after the diamond disappeared.

Clue 3: The Security Camera

The camera stopped recording ten minutes before the diamond was discovered missing. Investigators must determine whether this was an accident or sabotage.
